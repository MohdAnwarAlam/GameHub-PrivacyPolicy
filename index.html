Privacy Policy

App: Mini Games Hub (Android, package com.praveen.minigameshub)
Publisher: Anwar Alam
Effective date: 1 January 2027
Contact: alamanwar367@gmail.com

In short: Mini Games Hub works completely offline. It has no internet access, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. Everything the app remembers stays on your phone.

1. What the app stores on your device

To work properly the app saves the following in its own private storage on your device:

DataWhy
Settings (music and sound on/off, volumes, selected music track, vibration, theme)To remember your preferences.
Game progress (unlocked levels, saved games, best scores, play statistics)So you can continue a game and see your records.
Optional profile: a display name, an avatar choice, or a profile photo you pickTo personalise the app. The photo is chosen with the Android system photo picker; the app receives only that one picture and keeps a small cropped copy in its private storage.

This data never leaves your device. The app has no network permission, so it technically cannot send it anywhere.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, location, contacts, device identifiers, advertising ID, usage analytics or crash reports. The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, location or storage permissions.

3. Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs. It uses only open-source Android (AndroidX) and Kotlin libraries for its functionality. Because nothing is sent over the network, no data is shared with any third party. If you install the app from Google Play, Google may collect its own information as described in Google's privacy policy; that is outside this app.

4. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Settings → Reset game progress deletes levels, scores, saved games and statistics.
  • The Profile screen lets you change or remove your name, avatar and photo.
  • Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app, removes everything the app saved.

5. Children

The app is a family-friendly puzzle collection with no ads, no purchases, no chat and no links to other services, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a normal, non-rooted Android device. Because the app has no internet access, there is no transmission of your data to protect.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example by adding online features), this page will be updated first and the effective date above will change.
